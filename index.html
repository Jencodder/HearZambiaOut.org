Welcome to Hear Zambia Out

Answer real world academic questions and learn together as a Zambian community

Hear Zambia Out is a youth led advocacy movement created to bridge the gap between youth opinions and national decision making.It stands as a high powered program that promotes critical thinking and analysis on issues affecting national matters. It is set up to train the youths of Zambia to develop critical problem solving abilities and intellectual application of youthful knowledge on national and international matters. Hear Zambia out provides a safe, open and innovative space where youths of Zambia can express there opinions, generate solutions and influence policies that shape tegre future.

Founded on the belief that youth are not just beneficiaries of change but leaders of change. Hear Zambia Out empowers youths with the tools, knowledge and right digital platforms to participate meaningfully in governance, national dialogue and public policies. Through online polls and physical Policy Hackathons. This is to promote accountability, credibility, meaniful citizenship and Cooper between youths and youth led government programs.

OUR PURPOSE

Hear Zambia Out exists to transform the landscape of national decision-making by ensuring the perspectives of young Zambians are not just heard, but are instrumental in shaping our collective future. We are dedicated to cultivating a generation that is not only informed and confident but also empowered to directly influence the policies that define their lives—from the classrooms and workplaces of today to the technological and governance systems of tomorrow.

Through a dynamic fusion of digital engagement and grassroots outreach, we forge connections among young creators, advocates, and innovators. Our core mission is to channel this collective energy into co-designing tangible, impactful solutions for Zambia's most pressing challenges in education, employment, mental health, and social development.
